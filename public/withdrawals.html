Withdrawals

This operational policy is a launch template. Final timing, limits, identity checks, fees, and legal holds must be approved and published before accepting customer funds.

Request

Withdrawals must be requested from an authenticated account to a verified destination. We may request additional identity, source-of-funds, or fraud-prevention checks.

Timing and availability

Requests are processed only against settled, available balances. Pending funding, open allocations, legal holds, market closures, and provider outages may delay processing.

Fees and limits

Any network, provider, conversion, minimum, maximum, or expedited-processing fee must be shown before confirmation. Never represent a withdrawal as guaranteed until settlement is confirmed.

Support

Publish the official support contact, escalation process, and complaint-handling procedure before launch.
