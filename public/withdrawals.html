NOVABRIDGE

Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests are reviewed against the available balance on the account.

Request

Withdrawals must be requested from an authenticated account to a verified destination. We may request additional identity, source-of-funds, or fraud-prevention checks.

Timing and availability

Requests are processed only against settled, available balances. Pending funding, open allocations, legal holds, market closures, and provider outages may delay processing.

Fees and limits

Network costs may apply. A request is not complete until it is settled.

Support

Questions about a request can be sent from the email on the account.
