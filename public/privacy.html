Privacy Policy

This policy describes how NovaBridge handles account information.

Information collected

We collect identity and contact details, account credentials, funding and activity records, device and security data, and communications needed to operate the platform.

Use and sharing

We use information to provide accounts, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, maintain records, provide support, and improve security. We share information only with service providers, financial and compliance partners, authorities, or as otherwise disclosed.

Retention and rights

Records are retained for operational, legal, tax, audit, and dispute purposes. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection.

Security and contact

We use access controls, encryption in transit, logging, and monitoring. Privacy requests can be sent from the email on your account.
