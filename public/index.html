The mission

After a scam, or before a loan

EtherGrant is for people in the United States and around the world who were hit by a crypto scam, or who want to understand a crypto-backed loan before they sign. You can write a report note, see what collateral actually buys, and read a grant or an airdrop without sending anything. This site does not recover stolen funds and does not lend money.

Give a clear answer back

People keep meeting the same two pitches: someone will recover what a scam took, or someone will open a crypto-backed loan if you pay first. EtherGrant answers both with the step you can actually take.

A report goes to the offices that take them. A loan is collateral, a rate, and a price where that collateral can be sold. Awareness is being able to tell those apart. This page does not move funds and does not speak for any agency or foundation.
